Preparation:
Find a comfortable position, gently close your eyes if you wish, and prepare to release the noise and tension of the day. Breathe slowly and deeply.

1. External Relaxation:
We begin by relaxing the muscles of the body. Relax your forehead, cheeks, jaws, tongue, and throat. Let your eyes fall heavy. Feel your neck loose, your shoulders and arms falling heavy. Relax your chest, abdomen, legs, and feet. Feel your entire body completely loose and at peace.

2. Internal Relaxation:
Now we relax internal tensions. Feel your chest inside, relax your lungs, and calm your heart. Feel your stomach and intestines, releasing any knots, tension, or tightness. Notice your soft, warm, and luminous interior, like a refuge of peace.

3. Mental Relaxation:
Finally, relax your mind. Let worries and thoughts pass by like clouds in the wind. Do not dwell on any of them. If an image appears, gently let it go. Feel your mind silent, empty, and peaceful, like a crystal-clear lake in absolute stillness.

Peaceful State Ready:
You have reached a state of calm, silence, and coherence. You are in the best condition to begin your reflection on the Golden Rule. Whenever you are ready, continue to the exercise.
